Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2025

TripAI ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the TripAI mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information to provide and improve our services:

  • Photos: Images you capture within the app for landmark recognition. These are uploaded to our server for AI analysis.
  • Account information: If you sign in with Google, we collect your name, email address, and profile photo.
  • Device identifier: A unique device ID used to associate your scans with your session when using the app as a guest.
  • Location data: With your permission, we use your GPS coordinates to provide nearby landmark suggestions.
  • Usage data: Landmark scan history and saved items stored in your journal.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To analyze photos and identify landmarks using AI
  • To display your scan history and travel journal
  • To provide personalized nearby landmark suggestions
  • To improve the accuracy and performance of our service

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secure servers. Photos are retained to enable you to revisit your landmark history. You may delete your data at any time from within the app.

4. Third-Party Services

TripAI uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Gemini API: For AI-powered landmark analysis
  • Google Sign-In: For optional account authentication
  • Google Maps: For displaying nearby landmarks

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for advertising purposes. Data is shared with the above services solely to provide app functionality.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Use the app as a guest without creating an account

7. Children's Privacy

TripAI is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@mnaks.online.
