How it works
Three steps to explore the world
1

Open the app and point your camera

No setup needed. Launch SightAI, aim at any landmark, monument, or historic building, and tap the camera button.

2

AI analyzes the image

Our Gemini-powered engine identifies the landmark and generates rich historical content, key facts, and a narrative in your language.

3

Explore, listen, and save

Read the story, play the audio narration, discover nearby places, and save the landmark to your travel journal.
